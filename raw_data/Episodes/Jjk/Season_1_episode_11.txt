Although Nanami easily defeats Mahito, the curse escapes by reshaping his own soul to alter his body. 
Meanwhile, Yuji befriends Junpei over their shared love for movies. 
During dinner with Junpei and his mother, Yuji tells Junpei his belief that killing would diminish his ability to value human life and relationships. 
That night, Junpei’s mother is killed when a curse, drawn to one of Sukuna's fingers planted by Mahito, attacks their home. 
Mahito manipulates Junpei into believing that his former bully Shota Ito is responsible. 
As Mahito intends, a vengeful Junpei lures Yuji to the school just as he prepares to kill Ito.
